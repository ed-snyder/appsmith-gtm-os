# Synthesized Buyer Insights

## Common Pain Points (by frequency)
1. Engineers spending too much time on internal tools vs core product
2. Internal tool sprawl with inconsistent security and no version control
3. Business teams blocked waiting for engineering to build simple interfaces
4. Existing tools (Retool, etc.) too expensive at scale
5. Need to self-host for compliance/security requirements
6. AI tools lack context about internal business data

## Buying Triggers
- New compliance requirement (SOC 2, HIPAA) exposing internal tool gaps
- Engineering team scaling and needing to standardize tooling
- Business team requesting new internal tool (the straw that breaks the camel's back)
- Cost review revealing Retool/custom tool maintenance costs
- AI initiative from leadership looking for practical applications
- New CTO/VP Eng bringing platform engineering mindset

## Decision Process
- **Champion**: Usually a senior engineer or engineering manager who discovers Appsmith
- **Evaluator**: Engineering team does a POC (builds one internal tool)
- **Approver**: VP Eng or CTO for Business/Enterprise tier
- **Timeline**: 2-6 weeks from first sign-up to paid conversion (self-serve), 2-4 months for Enterprise
