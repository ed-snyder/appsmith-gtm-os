# Segment: Compliance Driven

## Segment Definition
Companies undergoing compliance certification (SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO 27001) or security audits that expose gaps in their internal tooling — lack of audit logs, RBAC, SSO, or self-hosting capability.

## Qualifying Criteria
- **Trigger**: Active compliance initiative (SOC 2 prep, HIPAA requirement, security audit)
- **Signals**: SOC 2 mentioned in job posts, compliance-related hiring, audit log feature searches
- **Industry**: Financial services, healthcare, enterprise SaaS (regulated industries)
- **Behavioral**: Searching for audit logs, SSO, RBAC, self-hosting documentation

## Estimated Volume + Sources
- ~3,000 accounts globally
- Source: Job board analysis for compliance-related roles + internal tools mentions
- Validation: SOC 2 certification databases, compliance conference attendee lists

## Evidence
- SOC 2 certification is increasingly required for B2B SaaS companies
- Internal tools are frequently flagged in security audits for lacking access controls
- Self-hosting is a common requirement for HIPAA-regulated data
- Audit log requirements drive upgrades from free to Business/Enterprise tiers

## Primary Pain Points
1. **No audit trail**: Internal tools built in-house lack audit logging
2. **Inconsistent access control**: No centralized RBAC across internal tools
3. **SSO requirement**: Security team mandates SSO for all business applications
4. **Data residency**: Compliance requires data to stay in specific geographies
5. **Vendor assessment**: Security team needs to evaluate and approve all tools

## Messaging Hooks
- "SSO, RBAC, audit logs, and self-hosting. Enterprise security without enterprise migration."
- "Pass the audit without rebuilding your internal tools from scratch"
- "Your auditor will love the audit logs. Your engineers will love the RBAC."
