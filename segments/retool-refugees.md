# Segment: Retool Refugees

## Segment Definition
Engineering teams currently using Retool who are experiencing cost pressure at scale and seeking an open-source alternative with self-hosting capabilities.

## Qualifying Criteria
- **Current tool**: Retool (detected via job posts, tech stack databases, or self-reported)
- **Company size**: 50-2,000 employees (cost pressure increases with builder count)
- **Pain signal**: Pricing complaints, procurement review, or contract renewal approaching
- **Tech signals**: Retool mentioned in job descriptions, BuiltWith/StackShare detection
- **Behavioral**: Visiting Appsmith pricing page, comparing Appsmith vs Retool content

## Estimated Volume + Sources
- ~5,000 accounts globally
- Source: Retool claims 20,000+ customers; estimated 25% are mid-market with cost sensitivity
- Validation: Job board scraping for Retool mentions, G2/Capterra review analysis

## Evidence
- Retool pricing increased significantly with their new plans ($50/builder Business)
- G2 reviews frequently cite "expensive" as a con for Retool
- Community forum posts asking about Retool alternatives increasing
- Appsmith vs Retool is a top search comparison query

## Primary Pain Points
1. **Cost at scale**: $50/builder/month adds up fast with 20+ builders
2. **Vendor lock-in**: Closed-source means no exit path if Retool changes direction
3. **Self-hosting limitations**: Retool VPC is Enterprise-only and complex
4. **Feature gaps for their use case**: Paying for features they don't use while missing ones they need

## Messaging Hooks
- "You're paying $50/builder for features you can get open-source"
- "What if you could self-host your internal tools platform for $15/user?"
- "Same internal tools, 70% less cost, full source code access"

## Recommended Angles
- Primary: **"Stop paying Retool tax"** (messaging/angles.md #1)
- Secondary: **"Own your stack, own your data"** (messaging/angles.md #3)

## Campaign Assignment
- Active: `retool-migration` campaign
- Channel mix: Email (primary), LinkedIn (secondary), comparison landing page (support)
