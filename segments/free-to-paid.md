# Segment: Free to Paid

## Segment Definition
Current Appsmith free tier users who are approaching plan limits or demonstrating usage patterns that indicate readiness for Business or Enterprise tier upgrade.

## Qualifying Criteria
- **Current plan**: Free tier
- **Usage signals**: 5 users reached (free limit), multiple apps created, API-heavy usage, workflow needs
- **Behavioral**: Visiting pricing page, attempting to access Business features, inviting 6th user
- **Company profile**: Matches ICP criteria (not hobbyists or students)

## Estimated Volume + Sources
- Dynamic (depends on current free user base)
- Source: Product analytics, CRM segmentation
- Validation: Product usage data cross-referenced with enrichment data

## Evidence
- Free-to-paid conversion is the primary growth motion for product-led SaaS
- Users who hit the 5-user limit are actively using Appsmith for team workflows
- Multiple app creation indicates platform adoption beyond a single use case
- API-heavy usage suggests integration into critical business processes

## Primary Pain Points
1. **User limit**: Team has grown beyond 5 free users
2. **Missing features**: Need workflows, custom roles, or audit logs (Business features)
3. **Security requirements**: RBAC, SSO, or compliance features required by their organization
4. **Support needs**: Moving from community support to needing SLAs and dedicated help
5. **Scale concerns**: Need better performance, managed hosting, or higher rate limits

## Messaging Hooks
- "Your team is shipping real tools on Appsmith. Time to unlock workflows, RBAC, and audit logs."
- "You've outgrown free — here's what Business unlocks for your team"
- "5 users building {{app_count}} apps deserves enterprise-grade features"

## Recommended Angles
- Primary: **"You've outgrown free"** (messaging/angles.md #5)
- Secondary: Feature-specific upgrade triggers based on usage patterns

## Campaign Assignment
- Active: `upgrade-nurture` campaign
- Channel mix: In-app messaging (primary), email (secondary), product-triggered notifications
