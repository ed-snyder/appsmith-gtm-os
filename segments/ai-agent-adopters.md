# Segment: AI Agent Adopters

## Segment Definition
Enterprise teams actively pursuing AI/agentic AI initiatives who need context-aware AI tools that integrate with their existing business applications (Salesforce, Zendesk, Slack) rather than standalone chatbots.

## Qualifying Criteria
- **Initiative**: Active AI adoption program or executive mandate
- **Tools**: Using Salesforce, Zendesk, Slack, or similar enterprise SaaS
- **Team**: Sales, support, or CS team of 10+ people
- **Signals**: AI budget allocation, ChatGPT Enterprise deployment, AI-focused job postings, AI vendor evaluations
- **Behavioral**: Visiting Appsmith Agents landing page, AI-related content engagement

## Estimated Volume + Sources
- ~10,000 accounts globally
- Source: Companies with AI-related job posts + enterprise SaaS stack + team size criteria
- Validation: LinkedIn job post analysis, technographic databases, AI conference attendee lists

## Evidence
- Enterprise AI adoption is accelerating (80%+ of enterprises have AI initiatives)
- Most AI deployments are generic chatbots that lack business context
- Teams are frustrated with AI tools that require switching between applications
- Chrome extension-based AI tools are gaining traction for in-context assistance

## Primary Pain Points
1. **Lack of context**: ChatGPT and generic AI don't know their customer data, product catalog, or internal processes
2. **Workflow disruption**: AI tools require leaving current application to use a separate interface
3. **Integration complexity**: Connecting AI to internal data sources requires significant engineering effort
4. **Security concerns**: Sending business data to third-party AI services raises compliance issues
5. **ROI uncertainty**: Hard to measure AI tool impact on team productivity

## Messaging Hooks
- "ChatGPT doesn't know your customer data. Appsmith Agents does."
- "AI that works inside Salesforce, Zendesk, and Slack — not in a separate tab"
- "Give your team AI that's trained on your business context, not the internet"

- Secondary: Pair with internal tools story for existing Appsmith customers
